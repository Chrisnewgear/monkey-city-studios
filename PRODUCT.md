# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primary users are startup founders and small business owners evaluating whether Monkey City Studios is the right partner for their digital initiatives. They typically arrive with a business goal in mind and need confidence in delivery capability before they commit to a conversation.

## Product Purpose
This product exists to help prospective clients quickly understand services, assess credibility, and start a project inquiry. Success looks like a clear path from first visit to contact, with enough trust-building information to motivate outreach.

## Brand Personality
The experience should feel bold and innovative, while remaining trustworthy and professional. The tone should also carry a premium, high-end quality that signals expert execution and reliable partnership.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS-template look
- Overly playful or cartoon styling
- Dark hacker/cyberpunk aesthetic
- Corporate stock-template minimalism

## Design Principles
- Lead with clarity: make service value and next actions understandable within seconds.
- Convert confidence into action: every section should support the decision to initiate contact.
- Show capability, not claims: reinforce trust with concrete signals of expertise and delivery quality.
- Keep the journey low-friction: reduce cognitive load and make inquiry pathways obvious.
- Balance ambition with credibility: modern and bold presentation should never compromise professionalism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No strict WCAG target is currently mandated, but practical accessibility defaults are required. Prioritize readable contrast, clear focus states, keyboard-friendly interactions, sensible semantics, and reduced-motion accommodations where motion is used.
